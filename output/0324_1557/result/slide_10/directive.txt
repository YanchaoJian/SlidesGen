### Design Directive for Slide Layout

---

#### [1. Global Settings]
1. **Create a Slide**:  
   - Use a blank slide layout.  

2. **Set Background Color**:  
   - Apply the main background color: `(245, 245, 245)` (light gray).  

---

#### [2. Static Decoration Layer (Static Elements)]
1. **Top Title Bar**:  
   - Create a rectangular shape at the top of the slide to serve as the title bar.  
   - Dimensions: `(left=0 inches, top=0 inches, width=10 inches, height=1.125 inches)` (occupies 20% of slide height).  
   - Fill color: `(0, 63, 135)` (deep blue).  
   - No border.  

2. **Footer Area**:  
   - Create a rectangular shape at the bottom of the slide for the footer.  
   - Dimensions: `(left=0 inches, top=6.375 inches, width=10 inches, height=1.125 inches)` (occupies 20% of slide height).  
   - Fill color: `(245, 245, 245)` (light gray).  
   - Add footer text:  
     - Content: "Slide 10 | Academic Blue Professional Theme".  
     - Font: Microsoft YaHei + Arial, 14pt, regular.  
     - Color: `(51, 51, 51)` (dark gray).  
     - Alignment: Right-aligned.  
     - Position: `(left=8 inches, top=6.75 inches)`.

---

#### [3. Dynamic Layout Layer (Dynamic Content Layout)]
1. **Title**:  
   - Add the slide title at the top of the page, centered within the title bar.  
   - Content: "Questions & Discussion".  
   - Font: Microsoft YaHei + Arial, 32pt, bold.  
   - Color: `(255, 255, 255)` (white).  
   - Position: Centered within the title bar `(left=0 inches, top=0.375 inches, width=10 inches, height=0.375 inches)`.  

2. **Body Content**:  
   - Create a text box for the main content below the title bar.  
   - Dimensions: `(left=0.5 inches, top=1.5 inches, width=9 inches, height=4.875 inches)` (occupies the remaining content area with 0.5-inch margins).  
   - Fill color: `(230, 240, 250)` (light blue).  
   - No border.  
   - Add the following text:  
     - "Thank you for your attention!"  
     - "Questions and feedback are welcome."  
   - Font: Microsoft YaHei + Arial, 18pt, regular.  
   - Color: `(51, 51, 51)` (dark gray).  
   - Alignment: Left-aligned.  

---

#### [4. Notes]
1. **Presenter Notes**:  
   - Add the following speaker notes:  
     - "Encourage audience engagement and discussion."  

---

### Summary of Key Design Adjustments:
- The title bar and footer were adapted from the style protocol to fit the "body page" layout, ensuring clarity and organization.  
- Decorative elements (e.g., geometric blocks) were minimized to avoid interference with the text content.  
- Content margins and alignment strictly follow the protocol for professional and structured presentation.  

This directive ensures brand consistency while adapting to the specific content type of the current slide.
