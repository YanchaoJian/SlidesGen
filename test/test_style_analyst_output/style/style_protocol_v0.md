## IV. Layout Principles

| Zone           | Y-range (px) | Height (px) | Description                                      |
|----------------|--------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------|

## V. Visual Features

### Decorative Elements
- **Header bar**: A horizontal bar in `#003F87` spans the top of the slide.
- **Summary box**: A rectangular section with a `#E6EDF4` background and rounded corners.
- **Keywords**: Buttons styled with `#003F87` background and white text.

### Shadow Effects
- No shadow effects.

### Border & Line Style
- Borders: Solid, `1px`, `#CCCCCC`.

### Shape Style
- Rounded rectangles dominate the design, with an 8px border radius.

---

## VI. Component Patterns

### Content Cards
- Background: `#E6EDF4`
- Border: `#CCCCCC`, 1px, rounded corners (8px radius)
- Shadow: No shadow

### Title Treatment
- Titles are bold and use the primary color (`#003F87`).

### Data Emphasis
- Keywords are styled as buttons with bold white text on a `#003F87` background.

### Icon Style (if visible)
- No icons present.

---

## VII. Design Quality Rules

- **Content fill ratio**: ~60% content, 40% whitespace.
- **Alignment**: Grid-aligned, with consistent spacing and margins.
- **Color contrast**: High contrast between text and background ensures readability.
